Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Kundan Speed Controller ("the extension") handles user information and permissions.

1. Single Purpose

The primary purpose of Kundan Speed Controller is to allow users to dynamically adjust playback rates for HTML5 videos embedded in standard web pages and nested iframes.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or browsing activity.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, emails, IPs, or device IDs.
  • No Browsing History: The extension does not track, record, or log any visited URLs, page titles, or search queries.
  • Local Storage Only: The extension uses chrome.storage.sync solely to save your chosen playback speed preference (e.g., 1.5x) across your own browser sessions. This value remains within your browser and is never transmitted to any external server.

3. Permissions Justification

  • <all_urls>: Required exclusively to detect standard HTML5 <video> elements and apply the selected playback rate across all domains and embedded third-party frames (such as LMS platforms, Moodle, and embedded players). No webpage content is extracted or sent anywhere.
  • storage: Required solely to persist user-selected playback speed settings locally in the browser.

4. Third-Party Services & Limited Use Disclosure

The extension contains no third-party tracking scripts, analytics, advertising networks, or external telemetry libraries. The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer directly at your support email address.
